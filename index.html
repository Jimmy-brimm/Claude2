How to Play

Controls

    Keys can be changed in Settings.

    Rules

    • One hit and you're sliced. Each slice is a point; slicing yourself or a teammate costs one.
    • Tap swing for a lunging slash that also swats boomerangs. Slash right out of a dash for a longer, wider dash-slash.
    • Hold throw to charge, release to throw. Move sideways as you let go to curve it.
    • It flies back to you. If it hits a wall it drops — walk over it, or hold throw empty-handed to recall it.
    • Dashing slips past thrown boomerangs and hops gaps. Throwing leaves you open for a moment. Hide in bushes. Don't fall in!
    • First to the target score wins. Ties go to a tie-breaker: first slice wins.
