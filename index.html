How to Play

Controls

    Keys can be changed in Settings.

    Rules

    • One hit and you're sliced. Every slice scores a point.
    • Tap your swing button for a close-range slash. Slashes also deflect boomerangs!
    • Hold aim to charge a throw — the longer you hold, the further it flies. It comes back to you, so catch it!
    • No boomerang, no attacks. Pick it up if it lands.
    • Dash to dodge. Hide in bushes. Don't fall in the water or the chasm.
    • First to the target score wins the match.
